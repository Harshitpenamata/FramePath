# Scenario pilot update — 7 October 2026

Framepath now begins with one of 176 real video tasks across 18 categories, mapped to 20 archetypes and 17 skill clusters. The existing Site, account data, legacy lessons, public audience and pricing are retained.

## Changes
- Nine-screen task diagnostic captures outcome, gear and control, time, device/bandwidth/accessibility, preferences and per-skill starting points; three adaptive visual checks. Meter changes on submission only. Skips remain unknown. Result editing is separate from a forward-only practical attempt.
- Pure TypeScript recipe engine creates scoped routes from approved JSON. Time, skill evidence and practice preferences change route composition, order and variants. Incompatible formats block saving; missing specialist conditions yield labelled rehearsals.
- Added 23 original teaching units and interactive demonstrations alongside the retained 16-pathway, 80-session skill bank. Watch/Make/Reflect, task-specific criteria, anchors, saved work and optional AI feedback are integrated with existing APIs.
- Expanded credited external resources from 36 to 50, with task, level, format, device and bandwidth filters and explicit source-verification notes.
- Added consent/safety guidance, safe-content declarations, image screening, bounded uploads, response recovery, offline notices and downloadable original lesson text.
- Preserved fixed two-pathway free access and allowances, ₹999 annual simulated checkout, simulated mentors, private records and consent-controlled portfolios.

## Validation
- TypeScript check; 2,112 scenario configurations covering all 176 uses; 432 legacy combinations.
- Ten synthetic personas through real local APIs, workspace and lesson rendering.
- Desktop/mobile layouts, submission-only meter, sequence gates, saved drafts, reflection scoring, replanning, ownership, quotas, upload failures, portfolio publish/unpublish and error recovery.
- Harmless image moderation succeeded. Live AI question and assignment review returned insufficient-credit responses without consuming learning allowances.

## Limits
- Runtime resolves a use case to its primary archetype; secondary archetypes are retained as cross-references.
- Learning routes produce scoped practice, not medical/legal/safety authorisation or professional certification.
- Video is not automatically watched, heard or moderated. AI feedback sees notes and at most one still; consent/privacy declarations and image moderation have limits.
- English-first; external accessibility and links vary. Offline support is downloaded text/open-page practice, without offline account synchronization.
- Test-only identity helpers and test records are local, excluded from publication. No database schema or dependency changes.
