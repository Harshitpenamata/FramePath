# Framepath beginner curriculum update

Source: the user-supplied **FramePath Flow.pdf**, reviewed on 6 October 2026. Page 1 is blank and the final Open decisions section is truncated; earlier pricing and pilot terms remain in effect.

## Learning contract

The beginner level has eight ordered pathways and forty sessions: Purpose and Seeing; Camera and Exposure; Composition and Shot Sizes; Light Basics; Sound; Stability, Movement and Coverage; Editing Fundamentals; Story Basics and Capstone. Each pathway repeats appropriate pre-production, production and post-production phases. Estimated total effort is 54 hours; a “week” is a curriculum unit, not a calendar lock.

`app/beginner-curriculum.ts` contains the immutable sequence, Think questions, tasks, evidence alternatives, minimum anchors, short retry tasks, practice suggestions and resource mappings. Stable new IDs are `basic-w1-0` through `basic-w8-4`. Original Explore and Craft identifiers and records are retained.

Every new beginner self-check uses weights 40/35/25. Completion requires at least 75/100 and every rating at least 2/4. Ratings are entered by the learner; neither AI nor the server claims to have independently graded a film. The server calculates the score and enforces sequential unlocks. Earned passes remain available. A labelled written or storyboard simulation can substitute for filming.

The first two beginner pathways include free practice, uploads and reflection. Other practice uses the existing ₹999 annual Explore entitlement. Checkout and mentors remain explicitly simulated. Public lessons can be previewed; account ownership controls saved work and media.

## Application changes

- Diagnostic captures a running subject, filming comfort, hours, 3/7/14-day suggested rhythm and practice style. The initial saved plan is transparently identified as a curriculum template.
- Eight-week map, weekly five-session pages, Learn / Make / Reflect sessions, focused retries and forty-session completion replace the short projects as the primary beginner journey.
- Session-five passes create/update one weekly work record. Eight weekly pieces can be selected for a portfolio; up to nine selections are supported. Public sharing still requires explicit consent.
- Five private journals and twelve downloadable original worksheets support ongoing practice.
- Optional capstone mentor choice uses the existing fictional profiles. Demo review uses six criteria, weights 25/20/15/20/10/10, approval at 70 with each at least 2. It does not gate Basic completion.
- `reflection_attempts` stores every new beginner attempt; state keeps the latest five for immediate display. Inserts and learner-state changes share a D1 transaction and revision check. The account export includes full history. Referenced media cannot be deleted as unused.
- Schema migration `0001_odd_pretty_boy.sql` adds the new table and owner/module/date index; the previous migration is unchanged.

## AI contract and limits

The existing Responses API integration receives the fixed curriculum, profile, current assignment and rubric, recent reflection, bounded journal excerpts and allowance context. AI may suggest examples, subject choices, practice tier and scheduling. It cannot write progress, change the curriculum, alter weights or award scores. Suggestions remain advice; the deterministic curriculum remains the executable plan. The older Craft sequence is separately preserved.

Only learner text and one explicitly selected still can be analysed. No video, audio or URL inspection is claimed. Failed API calls release their reserved allowance. Live integration testing on 6 October returned the provider's exhausted-credit condition and confirmed unchanged allowance. Successful live pedagogical responses must be checked after credits are added.

## Personalised brief and guided discovery (6 October follow-up)

`learner-brief.ts` derives a transparent brief from the saved diagnostic: goal, equipment, experience, time, filming comfort, practice mode and the three starting signals. The diagnostic report explains needs, the route through the three recurring phases and the final capstone, with a 54-hour estimate scaled to weekly availability. My Journey retains the same brief. The authored foundation sequence stays fixed; the report does not claim to be an LLM assessment or a certification.

`discovery-content.ts` adds forty authored hooks, choice-specific explanations, two concept labels and a worked example per session. `session-discovery.tsx` presents Try it → two ideas → In practice → Go deeper. Videos mount only in Go deeper while Learn is active; a full reading alternative is available. Make and Reflect retain their existing gates and scoring. Warm-ups are ungraded and never write progress. Learners can revisit or skip among teaching topics; returning from Make retains the current teaching topic.

Four small demonstrations support the teaching: simulated tonal separation, an information-framing diagram, a visual digital peak meter and keyboard-accessible shot reordering. These are labelled illustrations, not real image/audio analysis or camera controls. The personal example bridge uses the saved running subject and a goal/comfort-appropriate prompt.

Validation for this follow-up: all forty teaching flows, all choice feedback and topic transitions, personalised brief variations for product/editing/camera/story goals, video unmount when leaving Learn, reading fallback, anonymous warm-ups, preserved Make/Reflect gates, and mobile session-list controls. ## Visual lessons and AI assignment reviews (6 October, version 6)

The main beginner session now has two views: Lesson and Assignment. Production phases remain in the curriculum but no longer occupy a second navigation row. Simple session titles and a collapsed session list reduce repetition. Lessons use a large photographic experiment followed by short carousel cards and an optional video/reading card. Multiple-choice warm-ups are removed. Controls change the visible crop, focus, brightness, colour, format, titles, sequence or simulated motion/level. Depth comparisons and simulations are labelled; images are generated teaching examples. Three optimised WebP assets are served locally, with original images and prompts retained outside the deploy checkout.

The diagnostic summary is three concise cards (goal, starting point, route) with the action to save/start always outside the carousel. The learner's recorded goals, available time and equipment remain reflected in the plan.

Assignment reviews use /api/review and the existing server-side OpenAI connection. The first two sessions (basic-w1-0 and basic-w1-1) each receive two free reviews. Free practice remains available for the first two entire weeks. Later reviews use the existing shared annual feedback allowance. Once the two free reviews are used, members can use their annual allowance. No new payment or credential is created. Failures release the reservation. Atomic per-scope quotas, per-user/hour and global/day limits bound requests.

AI receives the fixed three-criterion rubric, learner notes and at most one owned image. It cannot watch video, hear audio or fetch viewing links. Structured output is validated on the server. Missing evidence produces null ratings and no total. When all ratings exist, the server calculates the existing 40/35/25 total and minimum-criterion rule. The learner explicitly saves the review to progress. Acceptance loads an owned, completed stored review; it rejects foreign, malformed, incomplete and duplicate reviews. Accepted attempts are labelled AI first pass and enter the same attempt history and weekly-project flow. Self-check is an explicit alternative, never labelled AI. Older review records are preserved.

This version intentionally supersedes the earlier restriction that AI could only advise on self-ratings: the new dedicated assignment-review route can provide provisional criterion ratings, with strict evidence limits and deterministic progression. The general-purpose AI assistant remains advisory. Saved review attachments cannot be removed as unused media.

Implementation follows the official Responses structured-output format: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs . Authentication, upload ownership, account revision checks, portfolios and the older Craft course remain on their existing contracts.
