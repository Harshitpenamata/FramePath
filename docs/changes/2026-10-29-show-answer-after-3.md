# Show the answer after three wrong tries

The slider exercises in the lessons (exposure, white balance, audio mix and framing) now offer a **Show me the answer** button after three wrong checks. It sets the sliders to a correct answer, says so ("Answer shown: ..."), and counts the exercise as done so a learner is never stuck. The count resets when the lesson page reloads.

Not covered (they already give a hint per wrong try): order, sort, pick and spot exercises.

File: `app/videography/tools.tsx`.
