# "Before you start" FAQ on every task (24 Oct 2026, pilot)

So learners don't spend time on the wrong thing, the Make tab now has a short FAQ under "How it's checked":

- **What exactly do I hand in?** Per task, written to fit the form (up to 3 photos or videos, or one link, plus a note).
- **Common mistakes:** 2–3 per task, the ones most likely to send a hand-in back.
- **What if I don't have the gear or the people?** The same answer everywhere: a phone is enough at Beginner, free
  editors, filming yourself or a friend, practice clients.
- **Can I hand in again?** Yes, each hand-in is a new piece; 2 AI reviews per module.

The first two are open by default. Content: `app/videography/task-faq.ts` (45 tasks), drafted by Claude for the team
to review; style tasks share it because the skill and the evidence are the same. No AI is used. Test: "every task has
its Before you start FAQ". An "Ask about this task" AI helper can follow after the pilot if the FAQ isn't enough.
