# End-of-pilot survey

Send it when a tester finishes their course, or at the end of the two weeks, whichever comes first.
Build it as a Google Form (no sign-in required, collect email off) and paste the form link into the messages below.
Seven questions, about 3 minutes. Questions 1–4 are required; the rest are optional.

## The questions

1. **How likely are you to recommend Framepath to a friend who wants to learn video?**
   0 (not at all) – 10 (definitely)
2. **How useful was Framepath for what you wanted to learn?**
   Not useful · A little useful · Useful · Very useful
3. **What device did you mostly use?**
   Android phone · iPhone · Laptop or desktop · A mix
4. **How far did you get?**
   Didn't start a lesson · Watched some lessons · Handed in at least one task · Finished a module or more
5. **What was the most useful part?** (short answer)
6. **What was confusing, frustrating or didn't work?** (short answer)
7. **Did the AI feedback on your work help you improve?**
   Didn't get any · Not really · Somewhat · Yes, a lot
   *Optional follow-up:* Anything else you'd like to tell us? (long answer)

Last screen: "Thank you. Your answers are anonymous unless you choose to add your name. They only go to the Framepath team."
