## New modules: Working with people

### B-PS · Work with people and present your work (beginner, 5 h)
- **Outcome:** Talk about your work clearly, listen well and agree next steps in writing.
- **Task — Your first client meeting** (written hand-in): Plan a short video for a real or practice client, like a shop, a friend or a club. Share the questions you asked, a summary of their answers (who it’s for, the goal and any limits), and the follow-up message you sent confirming what you’ll make, who does what and when.
- **What good looks like:** Your questions found the real goal, your summary matches what they said, and your follow-up is clear and polite.
- **AI review checks:**
  - Your questions are open and find the audience, goal and limits
  - Your summary matches what the client said
  - Your follow-up confirms what, who and when, politely

### I-PS · Win clients and pitch your ideas (intermediate, 8 h)
- **Outcome:** Find what a client really needs, pitch an idea, and handle price and revisions.
- **Task — Client pitch pack** (written hand-in): For a real or practice client, share your discovery questions and what you learned, a one-page treatment for your idea, your price and what it includes, and your reply to two revision notes that clash.
- **What good looks like:** Your idea solves the client’s real problem, your price is clear about what’s included, and your revision reply is calm and specific.
- **AI review checks:**
  - Discovery found the real audience, problem and goal
  - The treatment shows the idea, the look and why it fits
  - The price says what’s included and what isn’t
  - The revision reply finds the concern and proposes one plan

### A-PS · Lead teams and win approval (advanced, 10 h)
- **Outcome:** Lead a crew, handle hard client situations, price your value and pitch for approval.
- **Task — Leadership and pitch pack** (written hand-in): Share a crew briefing for a complex shoot, your plan for a client with clashing stakeholders and a fixed deadline, three service options with prices and outcomes, a short positioning statement, and the outline of a pitch that ends with a clear request.
- **What good looks like:** Your briefing makes roles and escalation clear, your client plan protects the deadline and the relationship, and your pitch builds to a clear request.
- **AI review checks:**
  - The crew briefing sets roles and how to raise problems
  - The client plan handles the clash and protects the deadline
  - The three options link price to outcomes
  - The pitch ends with a clear request
